## Ground rules used
- **Timeline:** 25 fps. Most Folder A clips are 25p. The 50p clips (565, 570, 572, 573, 575, 576) halve cleanly. The 30p iPhone clips (B) convert with an occasional dropped frame, which is invisible at these cut lengths.
- **Framing:** every chosen shot is natively vertical (2160×3840 for A; 2160×3840 or 1080×1920 for B). The base is a full-frame scale to 1080×1920. "Punch-in" means a digital zoom, and up to 2.0× stays at full resolution on 4K sources.
- **Dialogue first:** all on-camera dialogue below is **real captured audio**. Only 4 lines are new, and all of them are off-screen or voice-over (marked **NEW VO**).
- **Timestamp accuracy:**
  - Folder A in/outs are based on **word-level** automatic timing (±0.15 s).
  - Folder B in/outs are based on **segment-level** timing (±0.5 s), because the B originals haven't been downloaded yet.
  - All in/outs get frame-trimmed by ear/waveform at edit time.
- **Crew voices** (director cues, "Go", "Nice") are present on many clips. They are cut around or muted, as noted per beat.
